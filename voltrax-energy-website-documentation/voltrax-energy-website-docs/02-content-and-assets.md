## Product content required

For each product, obtain:

- Product name
- Category
- Brand/manufacturer, if known
- Clear product photograph
- Short description
- Important specifications
- Price or “Contact for price” preference
- Availability
- Featured status
- Search keywords

## Image requirements

- Logo: SVG preferred; transparent PNG accepted.
- Hero/product composition: supplied product images or licensed/owned imagery.
- Product images: ideally at least 1200 × 1200 px, clean background, consistent angle where possible.
- Format: WebP or AVIF for production; maintain source originals outside the deployed assets if desired.
- Naming: lowercase descriptive names such as `felicity-5kva-inverter.webp`.
- Do not take images from competitors’ websites without permission.

## Testimonials and trust content

Only include testimonials, customer logos, certifications, warranty promises and partner brands when the client supplies permission and accurate wording. If none are supplied, omit the section rather than fabricate social proof.
