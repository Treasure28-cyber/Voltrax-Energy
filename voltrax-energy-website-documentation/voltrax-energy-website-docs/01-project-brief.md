# 01 — Project Brief

## Project identity

**Client:** Voltrax Energy Nigeria Limited  
**Product:** Public business and product-catalogue website  
**Pages:** Home and Products  
**Primary conversion:** WhatsApp enquiry  
**Primary market:** Warri/Delta State and wider Nigerian market, subject to client confirmation

## Problem statement

Potential customers need a fast and trustworthy way to understand Voltrax Energy, discover its solar, electrical and security products, and contact the company without navigating a complicated online store. The client needs a professional web presence within a controlled budget.

## Goal

Create a simple, credible and visually polished website that introduces the business, makes products easy to discover, works very well on mobile devices, and moves qualified visitors into a WhatsApp conversation.

## Business objectives

- Establish an official `.com` web presence.
- Present the company as reliable and professional.
- Display major product categories and featured products.
- Generate product enquiries through WhatsApp and telephone.
- Support basic local search visibility.
- Allow inexpensive product updates through a local data file.

## User objectives

- Understand what Voltrax Energy offers within seconds.
- Browse products without creating an account.
- Search or filter the catalogue quickly.
- See important product specifications clearly.
- Ask about a particular product with minimal typing.
- Find the company’s contact and location information.

## Primary audiences

- Homeowners seeking alternative power solutions
- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Offices, schools and organisations
- Solar installers and electrical contractors
- Customers seeking CCTV/security products

## Success measures

- A visitor can identify the company’s offering from the first screen.
- A visitor can reach a relevant WhatsApp conversation within two deliberate actions.
- Product search and category filtering produce correct results.
- The site remains usable at 320 px width and scales cleanly to large desktop screens.
- Production pages load without console errors or broken assets.
- Search engines can crawl both public routes.

## Scope

### Included

- Home route: `/`
- Products route: `/products`
- Responsive header and mobile navigation
- Product search and category filters
- Featured products
- Product details presented through a modal, drawer, or accessible expandable view
- Product-specific WhatsApp links
- General WhatsApp contact link
- Telephone, email, address, map and social links where supplied
- Custom 404/not-found experience
- Basic technical SEO
- Static deployment configuration
- Up to 20 initial products unless the commercial agreement changes

### Excluded

- Admin dashboard or CMS
- Database or backend API
- User accounts or authentication
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Online payment
- Order history or tracking
- Delivery-price calculation
- Product reviews and wish lists
- Blog or news publishing
- Live-chat service
- Individual SEO product routes
- Unlimited post-launch product entry

## Constraints

- Development fee and scope are intentionally limited.
- Hosting should support a static React build and commercial use.
- Product content will be edited in source-controlled data files.
- Network conditions may be slow, so imagery and JavaScript must be economical.
- The client may not provide all content at the beginning; placeholders must remain obvious and easy to replace.

## Assumptions requiring confirmation

- Products are enquiry-only and are not purchased on the website.
- Customers do not need accounts.
- Prices may be hidden per product and replaced with “Contact for price.”
- One WhatsApp number receives all enquiries.
- English is the launch language.
- Product maintenance after launch is handled by the developer and priced separately.
